# 段永平投资逻辑提炼

This reference summarizes the user's PDF `段永平投资问答录(投资逻辑篇).pdf` for company analysis. Use these as operating rules, not decorative quotes.

## 一句话内核

- 买股票就是买公司。
- 买公司就是买未来净现金流的折现。
- 未来现金流折现主要是一种思维方式，不是靠公式精确算出来。
- 不懂不做是必要前提。看不懂就不碰，哪怕价格看起来便宜。

## 硬门槛

### 1. 看不懂不投

Only proceed when the analyst can explain:

- 公司到底靠什么赚钱。
- 客户为什么持续付钱。
- 竞争对手为什么长期抢不走。
- 未来 5-10 年的关键变量是什么。
- 最大风险在哪里，以及什么事实会证明原判断错了。

If these cannot be answered plainly, classify as `不懂，放弃` or `懂一点，继续观察`.

### 2. 先看商业模式

Judge whether the business is worth deeper work before studying price. Good signs:

- Long-term profit and net cash flow are strong.
- Profit pool is hard for competitors to take.
- Product or service has durable differentiation.
- The business does not require endless reinvestment just to stand still.
- The company can remain healthy if the stock market closes for 10 years.

Bad signs:

- Low price is the main attraction.
- Profit depends on financial engineering, subsidies, cycles, or opaque assumptions.
- Heavy capex, debt, or working capital consumes most reported profit.
- The analyst cannot explain unit economics and customer behavior.

### 3. 再看企业文化

Treat culture as a filter. A bad culture can be a complete reason not to invest.

Look for:

- Long-term health over short-term market pleasing.
- Customer/user orientation and product quality.
- Honest communication and consistency between words and actions.
- Rational capital allocation.
- Management behavior that would still make sense if the company were private.

Avoid:

- 急功近利.
- Management that caters mainly to market expectations.
- Aggressive accounting, repeated story changes, related-party opacity, empire building, or value-destructive acquisitions.
- Any company whose people or culture you would not want to deal with.

### 4. 财报用于排除，现金流优先

Financial statements are the first step and an exclusion tool. They rarely prove an investment by themselves.

Focus on:

- Debt and net cash.
- Operating cash flow and free cash flow.
- Real profit quality.
- Capex needs.
- Working capital stress: receivables, inventory, DSO, payables.
- Expense rationality.
- Goodwill and asset quality.
- Whether cash flow follows profit over time.

Use ROE/ROA as supporting evidence, not as a standalone screen. High ROE is more meaningful when not produced by excessive leverage.

### 5. 护城河来自生意模式和文化

Moat is not a slogan. Tie it to concrete evidence:

- Brand or product differentiation.
- Switching costs or network effects.
- Scale or cost advantage.
- Distribution or ecosystem advantage.
- Regulatory or license advantage, only if durable and not politically fragile.
- Culture and organizational ability that make the above last.

Ask whether the moat will still protect net cash flow 10 years from now.

### 6. 估值必须连接未来现金流

PE, PB, and recent price are reference points, not the investment standard.

Valuation should answer:

- What future cash flow path is the current price implying?
- What has to be true for that implied path to happen?
- What downside path would make the current price too expensive?
- Is the gap large enough to compensate for uncertainty?

Do not call something cheap just because PE is low. Do not call something expensive only because PE is high.

### 7. 机会成本必须出现

Every decision must compare against alternatives. Opportunity cost may be:

- Cash or short-term government bills.
- Broad index fund.
- A better-understood company.
- The user's existing holdings.
- Waiting for a clearly irrational price.

If the company is only "okay" versus available alternatives, the conclusion should usually be `继续观察` or `放弃`.

### 8. 买卖纪律

Buying:

- Buy only when the company is understandable and the price is clearly attractive versus value and opportunity cost.
- "Reasonable price" is often optional; clearly unreasonable cheapness is the opportunity.
- Past highs/lows are irrelevant.

Holding:

- Holding equals deciding again not to sell today. Recheck the business, not the original cost.
- Long-term holding only makes sense after buying the right business.

Selling or reducing:

- Do not sell because "already made money".
- Consider selling when the thesis is wrong, business quality deteriorates, price is far above conservative value, or a clearly better opportunity appears.
- Never let sunk cost or accounting profit/loss drive the decision.

## Red Flags Checklist

Stop or lower confidence if any of these are material:

- The business cannot be explained simply.
- Cash flow is persistently weaker than profit.
- Debt or refinancing risk can impair survival.
- Growth requires constant dilution or borrowing.
- Management prioritizes stock price optics over business health.
- Culture looks利益导向而非是非导向.
- Reported moat is just a high market share without customer stickiness or differentiation.
- The attractive case depends mainly on macro timing, policy speculation, chart patterns, or multiple expansion.

Use these exact section ideas, not necessarily exact wording:

- `看不懂不投`
- `现金流优先`
- `便宜不能替代理解`
- `持有等于重新买入`
- `机会成本必须出现`
