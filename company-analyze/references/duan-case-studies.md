# 段永平公开投资案例校准

Use this file to align company analysis with Duan Yongping's actual disclosed behavior. Treat it as a calibration aid, not a database of exact trades.

## Rules For Historical Reconstruction

- Separate `claimed buy date`, `known holding period`, and `public disclosure date`.
- Use the financial statements, price, industry facts, and risk facts available at or before the decision date.
- Do not use later stock returns, later revenue, later product success, or later public reputation to justify the original buy.
- If the date is approximate, write `约 YYYY` or `YYYY-YYYY` and lower confidence.
- If a source only proves he held or discussed a company, do not call it a buy.
- Record evidence quality:
  - `strong public record`: primary filing, direct post/interview, or reliable record of ownership.
  - `credible secondary report`: reputable media or biography-style source.
  - `from Q&A/PDF only`: user's PDF or mirrored Q&A, useful for reasoning but not independent trade proof.
  - `unverified`: mention only as a lead to research further.

## Case Notes

### NetEase

Public records say Duan was NetEase's second-largest individual shareholder from 2002 to 2004, with a peak ownership above 10%. NetEase's public history shows the company had a severe 2001 crisis, including alleged sales-data issues, executive departures, and weak market sentiment; it also formed NetEase Games in 2001 and launched Westward Journey Online in December 2001. This aligns with a Duan-style analysis where the crucial question is not "the stock fell a lot", but whether the surviving business could generate future cash flow that the market was missing.

Reconstruct with:

- NetEase 2001-2002 filings or annual reports.
- Nasdaq trading suspension and post-suspension price context.
- Cash, debt, real revenue, and whether game revenue was already visible.
- Ding Lei/founder incentives and governance risk.
- What facts were unknowable in 2002 and must not be used.

### AMERCO / U-Haul

AMERCO filed for Chapter 11 in June 2003 and emerged in March 2004; public sources also note the operating U-Haul business continued. If using this case, the analysis must distinguish operating business value from holding-company/legal/financing distress.

Reconstruct with:

- AMERCO 10-Ks before the bankruptcy and restructuring disclosures.
- Fleet/real-estate assets, rental economics, debt maturity, and liquidity.
- Whether operating cash flow survived the financial distress.
- What price implied about liquidation value and normalized earning power at that time.

### Apple

Duan's Q&A discusses Apple around the 2011-2012 period, including buying near then-high prices and focusing on future cash flow rather than past price. Public data available then showed Apple had a very large 2011 revenue/profit base, high iPhone contribution, and substantial cash reserves; Apple announced a dividend plan on March 19, 2012.

Reconstruct with:

- Apple FY2011 10-K and FY2012 filings if the decision date is after those filings.
- iPhone/iPad/Mac revenue mix at that date.
- Cash and marketable securities at that date.
- Ecosystem/customer behavior visible then, not later services growth.
- The opportunity cost in 2011/2012, such as cash, Treasury yield, S&P 500, or another understandable company.

### Moutai

Use Moutai as a simple-business and brand/moat benchmark only when the exact date is unknown. The analysis should focus on whether the company had visible pricing power, authentic demand, high cash conversion, and limited reinvestment needs at the relevant date.

Reconstruct with:

- The annual report immediately before the claimed analysis date.
- Ex-factory price, retail price, channel inventory, anti-corruption/regulatory facts, and production constraints available then.
- Operating cash flow, advance receipts/contract liabilities, capex, and net cash.
